🎯 Target Persona & Skill Gaps

Who we are really making this for. The market is not only developers defending their existing jobs — it is people stepping into the new roles AI is creating, learning Claude to become productive, self-reliant, and to run their own operations.

AD
Abdullah Dönmez
🛒 Amazon Seller & Business Operator · Non-developer Claude power user
in/abdullah-dönmez ↗

Abdullah runs an Amazon e-commerce business and uses Claude to improve his day-to-day operations — listings, customer messages, inventory and sales analysis, and process automation. He is not a software engineer; he is a business owner becoming productive with AI. He represents the broader market this course is built for: not developers clinging to their jobs, but people equipping themselves for the new roles AI is creating — and helping themselves grow from asking Claude questions to building their own workflows.
