Ways of Working · Instructional Design

🎯 Tell · Show · Do · Apply

A four-beat instructional design loop that turns a raw script into a course module: tell the subject, show it visually, do it live on screen, then hand the camera to the audience.

📖
Tell
Read the script.

Turn the camera 180° on yourself and implement.
01

📖 Tell

Narrate

Read the script out loud, working through it sentence by sentence. This is the narration beat — checking timing, pacing, and flow while laying out what the subject is and why it matters.

1

Read the script

Perform a verbal run-through line by line to check natural timing and flow.

02

🖼️ Show

Understand

As each sentence is read, generate a context-specific image with Gemini so the subject is visualized the moment it is spoken. The goal of this beat is comprehension and recall — by the end you understand and remember what the subject is all about.

1

Generate images with Gemini

For each sentence, create a matching visual so the idea is anchored to a concrete image.

2

Understand & remember

Process the subject one sentence at a time until the topic is fully internalized.

03

🖥️ Do

Demonstrate

With the subject understood, switch to the screen share and perform the hands-on implementation. Build the thing live and record the screen shares so every step is captured exactly as it happens — the recording is the process.

1

Screen-share the implementation

Walk through the hands-on build live, in the real tools, end to end.

2

Record the screen shares

Capture every step on screen so the demonstration becomes reusable course footage.

3

Build a POC proof-of-concept project

Create a concrete proof-of-concept project addressing specific exam questions — publish it to GitHub so students see a real, working reference they can fork and run.

04

🎥 Apply

Activate

Close the loop by motivating the audience to act. The call to action is to turn the camera 180° onto themselves and implement what was just demonstrated. They stop watching and start doing — applying the subject in their own environment.

1

Motivate the audience

Make it obvious they can do this themselves — the bar is reachable.

2

Turn the camera 180°

Point the camera back at themselves and implement what was shown, hands-on.

3

Record cohort sessions & gather feedback

Run live cohort sessions, record them, and ask students targeted questions about their pain points and concerns. Map their feedback directly into the video course production so the content evolves with real learner needs.

🧑‍🚀

The Role of the Human

Transformation

AI runs the loop, but the human carries the pain and the joy of the process. They take the exam, overcome the fears, pass it, and then share the journey — documenting the steps so the next person gets onboarded with ease.

1

Feel the pain & the joy

The human lives the real struggle and reward of the process — not a passive viewer.

2

Take the exam & overcome the fears

Face the test, push through the fear, and pass it for real.

3

Share the journey & help others

Document the steps so others get onboarded with ease, following the path already walked.

🧾 The certificate is a receipt. It is not proof of plain watching — it is evidence of a transformational change that actually happened.
★

💎 Value Proposition

Why it works

Rifat's Delivery Pilot method runs the whole loop with AI assistance: AI builds the script and outline, AI generates the images, AI builds the hands-on implementation, and AI publishes the Apply section for the audience. It is an AI-assisted self-learning process — the learner gets certified, with the instructor used at the start to check the work against reality — and it targets the audience with popular AI certifications.

  • AI builds the script and outline (Tell).
  • AI generates the images (Show).
  • AI builds the hands-on implementation (Do).
  • AI publishes the Apply section for the audience (Apply).
  • An AI-assisted self-learning process that ends in certification.
  • Reality-checked with the instructor at the start, then audience-led.
  • Targets the audience with popular AI certifications.
