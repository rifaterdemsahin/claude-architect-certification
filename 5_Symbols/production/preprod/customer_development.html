📈 Customer Development

Personal Brand Customer Development

A step-by-step roadmap for building the AI Certification Course audience, validation loops, and company framework.

🔁 Iteration v1

v1: First-pass map translating personal brand authority into product-market validation and company building.

🔁 Iteration v2

v2: Refined iteration sharpening the validation loops and company-building flow.

1 Customer Discovery 📍 ACTIVE STAGE

Mindset: Jab, Jab...
  • Consolidate to Core Tasks

    Identify the primary problem and outline persona development. Who is the system architect needing AI validation?

  • Test MVP (Video Series)

    Build and distribute a high-value YouTube series as a fast MVP to gauge audience resonance and engagement.

  • Initial Funnel Setup

    Direct viewers towards organic traffic systems and initial signup paths.

  • Pivot Check

    Continuously review incoming responses. If we hit >40% average watch-time retention by week 4, continue; else pivot organic traffic strategies.

2 Customer Validation

Mindset: Jab, Jab...
  • Prosumer Input

    Gather explicit input from highly engaged user-advocates (prosumers) who participate in pilot tests.

  • Define Persona & PMF (Pilot)

    Prove Product-Market Fit with a targeted pilot group. Deepen understanding of persona pain points.

  • Map Business Model

    Clarify how pricing, memberships, and content scaling interact to create a sustainable cycle.

  • Scale Sales & Marketing

    Utilize paid memberships (LinkedIn, Subscribes) to fuel ongoing outreach, validating financial commitment.

3 Customer Creation

Mindset: Right Hook
  • Value Delivery

    Deliver the core value proposition of self-learning recorded models over standard paper certificates.

  • Maximize Value & Scale Operations

    Standardize operational flows to increase productivity and maintain high-fidelity output.

  • Increase Content Output

    Double down on content generation to build awareness at scale and secure the "Right Hook" monetization.

4 Company Building

Mindset: Long-Term Growth
  • Scale Infrastructure

    Transition from lightweight tools to durable infrastructure (Supabase, Fly.io, Azure Key Vault).

  • Systemize Operations

    Document playbooks and establish standard operating procedures for continuous delivery.

  • HIRE (Team & Contractors)

    Establish a core team and trusted contractors to handle logistics, support, and administrative overhead.

  • Support deliverypilot.net

    Establish robust support for customers and logistics, closing the feedback loop.
