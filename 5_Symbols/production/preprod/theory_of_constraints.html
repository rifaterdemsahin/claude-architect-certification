⚖️ Theory of Constraints

Customer Development Analysis

A critical review of the Customer Development process, identifying the core bottlenecks and sequencing risks in the current plan.

🚨

The Core Constraint: Problem in June 2026

The primary bottleneck is audience retention. As of June 2026, the course has been moved and is being given for free until the YouTube partnership goes active. This partnership activation represents the completion of the MVP, requiring 4,000 watch hours. Before optimizing monetization or scaling infrastructure, the system must first keep viewers engaged to hit this 4,000-hour milestone.

"This is your Customer Development page for the AI Certification Course — a clean mashup of Steve Blank's four-step model (Discovery → Validation → Creation → Company Building) with Gary Vee's 'Jab, Jab, Right Hook.' The framing is coherent and the v1→v2 iteration shows the thinking is live rather than decorative. A few observations worth flagging."

✅ What Works

The four stages are correctly ordered and the Jab/Right-Hook overlay maps sensibly — give value through Discovery and Validation, monetise at Creation.

Treating the YouTube series as the MVP is the right instinct: it's the cheapest possible test of audience resonance before building anything heavier.

⚠️ Kill Criteria & Financial Goals

The biggest risk was operating without kill criteria or defined financial thresholds. The exact numbers dictating the pivot or kill decision are now defined:

Financial Target: 10k monthly revenue is the ultimate business goal. From the lifetime of each individual course, the goal is 10k. After setting up the business, new certification courses will be added to reach the monthly goal.

Kill Criteria: The project should be killed if it fails to reach the MVP completion of 4,000 watch hours (YouTube partnership) or if subsequent courses consistently fail to reach the 10k lifetime revenue model, making the 10k monthly goal impossible.

🔥 Sequencing Risk

Stage 4 — Scale Infrastructure (Supabase, Fly.io, Key Vault), hire team and contractors, systemise ops — is a long way ahead of where the business actually sits at ~700 subs and ~£10K lifetime revenue.

That's fine as a horizon, but the page presents all four stages at equal weight, with no "you are here" marker. Given the scaffolding-deadlock pattern we've talked about before, a roadmap that gives this much real estate to infrastructure and hiring is itself a small temptation toward building over shipping.

This very page is arguably Stage 1 work dressed as Stage 4 planning.

🎯 Concrete Edits Implemented

To mitigate these risks, the following explicit constraints and markers have been added to the process:

1. Explicit Metric + Threshold
Added an explicit metric and threshold to the Pivot Check: "if 40% watch-time retention by week 4, continue; else pivot". This serves as the early-warning system before hitting the 4,000 watch-hour MVP completion.

2. Active Stage Marker
Put a visible marker showing which stage is active right now (Stage 1), so the later stages read as future, not as a to-do list competing for attention today.

3. The 200 Memberships Test Case
If the process works, while creating new courses, giving out the first 200 memberships will serve as the initial test case. This provides the ability to fix and optimize retention for the audience, using these retention metrics as validation before moving on to the Customer Creation stage.
