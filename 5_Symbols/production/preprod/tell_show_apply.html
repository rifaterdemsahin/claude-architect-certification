Ways of Working · Teaching Loop

🎯 Tell · Show · Apply

A three-beat teaching loop that turns a raw script into a course module: understand the subject, build it live on screen, then hand the camera to the audience.

📖
Tell
Read the script & generate images with Gemini, sentence by sentence.
→
🖥️
Show
Screen-share the hands-on implementation and record it.
→
🎥
Apply
Turn the camera 180° on yourself and do it.
01

📖 Tell

Understand

Read the script out loud and work through it sentence by sentence. As each line is read, generate a context-specific image with Gemini so the subject is visualized the moment it is spoken. The goal of this beat is comprehension and recall — by the end you understand and remember what the subject is all about.

1

Read the script

Go line by line, reading out loud to check timing, pacing, and flow.

2

Generate images with Gemini

For each sentence, create a matching visual so the idea is anchored to a concrete image.

3

Understand & remember

Process the subject one sentence at a time until the topic is fully internalized.

02

🖥️ Show

Demonstrate

With the subject understood, switch to the screen share and perform the hands-on implementation. This is the demonstration beat: build the thing live and record the screen shares so every step is captured exactly as it happens — the recording is the process.

1

Screen-share the implementation

Walk through the hands-on build live, in the real tools, end to end.

2

Record the screen shares

Capture every step on screen so the demonstration becomes reusable course footage.

03

🎥 Apply

Activate

Close the loop by motivating the audience to act. The call to action is to turn the camera 180° onto themselves and implement what was just demonstrated. They stop watching and start doing — applying the subject in their own environment.

1

Motivate the audience

Make it obvious they can do this themselves — the bar is reachable.

2

Turn the camera 180°

Point the camera back at themselves and implement what was shown, hands-on.
