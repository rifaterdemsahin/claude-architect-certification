Problem–Solution Fit

Do real people have this problem, and does our proposed solution actually relieve it?

“System architects can’t prove practical AI delivery skill — paper certificates don’t reflect real capability. Does a recorded, self-learning certification solve that pain?”

🔍 Validate the problem

🧩 Confirm the Pain Is Real

  • Define the persona & core job

    The system architect who needs to demonstrate hands-on AI delivery, not memorized trivia.

  • Run customer-discovery interviews

    Talk to the audience before building. Capture the pain in their own words.

  • Ship an MVP (YouTube series)

    A high-value video series is the fastest probe to see if the problem resonates.

💡 Validate the solution

🛠 Confirm the Solution Resonates

  • Test the value proposition

    Recorded, proof-of-work certification > standard paper certificates — does it land?

  • Gather prosumer feedback

    Engaged advocates pilot the format and tell us what’s missing.

  • Pivot check

    If watch-time retention < 40% by week 4, refine the message or pivot the format.

Product–Market Fit

A defined market pulls the product out of our hands — demand outpaces our ability to supply.

“Are people paying, returning, and referring — and would they be very disappointed if the certification disappeared?”

📈 Build the engine

🚀 Scale What Works

  • Lock the business model

    Align pricing, memberships, and content cadence into a repeatable, sustainable loop.

  • Scale sales & marketing

    Paid memberships (LinkedIn, subscriptions) validate real financial commitment.

  • Standardize delivery

    Productionize the pipeline so output stays high-fidelity as volume grows.

🩺 Measure the pull

📊 PMF Signals to Watch

  • ≥ 40% of users would be “very disappointed” without it (Sean Ellis test)
  • Organic referrals and word-of-mouth growth
  • Strong retention & repeat engagement cohort-over-cohort
  • Demand for enrollment outpaces our supply capacity
  • Falling CAC as reputation compounds
