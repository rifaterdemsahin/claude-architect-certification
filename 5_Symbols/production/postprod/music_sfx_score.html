🎚️ Global Audio Palette
Recurring elements used across every module — score these once, reuse everywhere for a consistent sonic identity.

🎬 Show Bumpers

  • Intro sting — 3s branded logo whoosh + tonal hit
  • Section transition — short whoosh between cues
  • Outro / CTA bed — warm pad under subscribe card

❓ IVQ Sound Kit

  • Question sting — curious rising 2-note motif
  • Correct answer — bright confirm ding
  • Incorrect — soft low buzz (non-punitive)

🪧 Overlay & Lower Third

  • Lower-third reveal — subtle slide + tick (every video)
  • Highlight pop — soft UI pop on emphasis
  • Diagram draw — pen/swish as elements appear

⌨️ Screen-Share Foley

  • Keyboard typing — light mechanical, low in mix
  • Code execute — soft enter/run tone
  • Success ding / error buzz for pass/fail
