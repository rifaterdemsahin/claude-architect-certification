👑 What Is the "Membership Paywall"?

Channel Memberships are the recurring paywall — viewers pay a monthly fee for perks the public doesn't get. This is the engine behind a course community.

👑

Channel Memberships

Viewers pay a recurring monthly price (you set tiers) for loyalty badges, custom emoji, and members-only videos, posts, and live chats. This is the paywall the certification cohort lives behind.

💖

Super Thanks / Chat

One-off tips on videos (Super Thanks) and highlighted paid messages in live streams (Super Chat). Lower-commitment ways for fans to fund the channel without subscribing to a tier.

💰

Ad & Premium Revenue

Only at full YPP (1,000 subs + 4,000h): a share of ad revenue plus a cut of YouTube Premium watch time. This is passive income on top of the direct paywall.

📣 The Marketing That Triggers It

£10,000 doesn't arrive from a paywall sitting quietly. It takes a 12-week attention campaign across two channels feeding one funnel: YouTube Shorts for reach, LinkedIn for high-intent architects.

🎬

YouTube Shorts — Reach

3 Shorts / week × 12 weeks = 36 Shorts. Each a 30–60s hook carved from a module (one sharp idea + a "join to go deeper" CTA). At ~2,000 avg views → ~72,000 Short views; at a 1.5% subscribe rate that's ~300–500 new subscribers — enough to clear the 1,000-sub gate. Shorts views also bank toward the Shorts monetization gate.

💼

LinkedIn — High Intent

3 posts / week × 12 weeks = 36 posts. Build-in-public progress, certification proof, and architecture takes aimed at the RAISE / architect audience. At ~1,500 impressions/post → ~54,000 impressions; at a 2% click-through that's ~1,000 channel visits from buyers who already trust Erdem.
