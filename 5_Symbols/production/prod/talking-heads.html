All on-camera presenter scenes extracted from the master script — with greenscreen setup and recording tips.

🟢 Greenscreen Recording Guide

💡 Lighting

  • Use a 3-point lighting setup: key (45°), fill (opposite 45°), back/hair light
  • Softboxes or diffused LEDs — avoid hard shadows on face
  • Keep green screen evenly lit — no hot spots or shadows
  • Subject at least 1.5m away from the green screen to avoid spill

👕 Wardrobe

  • No green, neon, or reflective clothing (will key out)
  • Solid colours preferred — avoid busy patterns or thin stripes
  • Dark tops (navy, charcoal, black) work best for keying
  • No shiny jewellery or glasses with anti-reflective coating

📷 Camera Setup

  • Resolution: 4K (3840×2160) at 24fps or 30fps
  • Eye level with camera — not looking up or down
  • Frame: head and shoulders (rule of thirds)
  • Manual white balance — do not use auto

🎤 Audio

  • Lavalier mic preferred (Rode Wireless Go II or similar)
  • Microphone 15–20cm from mouth, off-centre to avoid plosives
  • Record room tone for 30 seconds (noise floor sample)
  • Monitor levels — keep peaks at -6dB to -3dB

🎬 Delivery

  • Speak at conversation pace — not rushed, not too slow
  • Pause between sentences for easier editing
  • Look into the lens — not at the screen or prompter
  • Reset and re-record flubs — don't try to edit around them

🎞️ Post Keying

  • Use OBS Key Filter (built-in) or DaVinci Resolve 3D Keyer
  • Add Spill Suppression to remove green reflection on skin
  • Apply slight blur (0.5–1px) to key edges for natural blend
  • Place background image at correct aspect ratio (16:9)
